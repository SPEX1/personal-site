Flashy

Description

Flashy is my first ever project. I made it because I had to transition quickly from learning the basics in theory to applying what I learned in real projects. When you look closely into the repository, you will find a lot of "@ts-ignore".

It features the ability to create and save cards, it taught me a lot about saving stuff into the browser cache or into files. While that helped me a lot, the most notable thing was running into real problems which taught me more than just working after a strict workflow.
